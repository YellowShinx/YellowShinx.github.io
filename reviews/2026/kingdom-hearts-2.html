Reviewed on August 17, 2026

PROS
  • Awesome controlls.
  • Phenomenal soundtrack composed by Yoko Shimomura.
  • World variety.
  • Endgame content (Data Organization XIII, Lingering Will).
CONS
  • The prologue (Roxas's segment) can feel a bit slow on replay.
  • The second visit to many worlds can feel a bit forced.

Introduction & First Impressions

I could write a million things about this game and none of those would be objective. I got this game for the PS2 after playing Chain of Memories for a whole year on repeat. When my aunt got it for me, I didn't have a memery card on my PS2 so I replayed the prologue at least five times and I loved every second of it. Needless to say, when my parents finally bought me a memory card on Christmas, I came back to school having finished the game twice.

And thus, it is not a rare sight checking my Steam profile and watching me playing the 1.5 + 2.5 collection. And this little jorunal/website will have another entry for this game when it releases on Switch 2 this fall. It is one of my favourite games of all time, not because it's perfect, it's the sentimental value what this game has.

Gameplay and Combat Depth

The introduction of the **Reaction Command** system and the versatile **Drive Forms** (Valor, Wisdom, Limit, Master, and Final) added unprecedented layers of strategy to every encounter. Standard mob fights are fast-paced clearing puzzles, while boss battles—particularly against members of Organization XIII—demand precise blocking, reflexes, and pattern recognition.

The level design and world integration also improved significantly, giving players vibrant spaces to explore, even if some corridors feel slightly linear by modern standards. The flow-motion and agility keep traversal engaging throughout the entire 30-to-40-hour journey.

Final Verdict

Kingdom Hearts II is an absolute masterpiece of its era that holds up remarkably well today. Whether you are tackling it on standard difficulty or pushing your limits on Critical Mode, the combat satisfaction rarely drops. A mandatory experience for any action RPG fan.
